## Background & Evolution

Cloud computing has been around for over two decades, with the first cloud-based services emerging in the early 2000s. Since then, the technology has evolved significantly, with the rise of infrastructure as a service (IaaS), platform as a service (PaaS), and software as a service (SaaS). However, despite its many benefits, cloud computing has also faced several challenges, including security concerns, scalability issues, and high costs. The integration of AI into cloud computing has the potential to address these challenges, by providing a more efficient, scalable, and secure way of delivering IT services.
